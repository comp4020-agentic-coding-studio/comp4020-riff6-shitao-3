# Instruments for Mark-Making

Week 5 --- Time inside the instrument

---

## Weeks 1 through 4, in one sentence

A pure function of input: touch it, something happens; stop touching it,
nothing does.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# Ink doesn't wait for you to decide it's dry

Neither does a held note wait for you to decide it's over.

Giving an instrument its own advancing clock is what turns it from a
responsive surface into a small dynamical system.

---

## What that buys you

- state that can drift
- state that can oscillate
- state that can get stuck --- in a way no single input event caused

---

## This week's actual warning

Once decay and input both write to the same piece of state, a value can get
pushed to a boundary by one path while looking, from the outside, like it's
simply obeying the other.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> Master the stroke and method follows.

A clock is one more thing the stroke now has to answer to --- not a feature
bolted on beside it.

---

## Testing for it

Don't take one screenshot and trust it. Continue acting on the instrument
after the suspicious state is reached, and watch what it actually does next.

---

## Before studio 5

Bring a build where every change on screen is still a direct response to
input. Today it learns to keep moving without you.
