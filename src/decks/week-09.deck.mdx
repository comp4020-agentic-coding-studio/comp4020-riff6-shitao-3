# Instruments for Mark-Making

Week 9 --- When failure is the material

---

## Generative art has a history of this

- Vera Molnár's plotter drawings lean on the machine doing something
  slightly other than commanded
- circuit-bent and glitched instruments treat a malfunction as a found sound,
  not a defect to route around

---

# None of that is licence to ship whatever breaks first

---

## The one-sentence test

Can you say, in one sentence, what a viewer is meant to feel when the
failure happens --- and is that better than the instrument simply working?

If the answer is no: it's a bug. Fix it, the same as any other week.

---

## Why week 9, not week 1

- six weeks of debugging real, accidental failures first
- "this break is deliberate" has to be a claim you can back up by contrast
- not a cover story for something you didn't get around to fixing

---

## Before studio 9

Bring two things: one place your instrument currently breaks by accident,
and, if you have one, a place it could break on purpose instead.
