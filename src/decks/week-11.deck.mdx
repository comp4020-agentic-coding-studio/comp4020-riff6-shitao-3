# Instruments for Mark-Making

Week 11 --- Showing an instrument to a stranger

---

## Every studio so far has had you in the room

"Try moving the pointer faster." "Hold the key down." An exhibition removes
that person.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# It has to teach itself in the first few seconds

Or it teaches the viewer nothing, and they move on.

---

## A much stricter bar than "works when I use it"

Run the instrument unattended for two minutes and see what state it's
actually in. Failure modes that never show up in a five-minute demo:

- a listener that never unregisters
- a decay term that reaches a stuck boundary and stays there
- a keyboard focus requirement nobody wrote down

---

## Staging is the other half

- what does a viewer need physically --- a mouse, a touchscreen, both hands
  free
- what, if anything, do they need told before they touch it

The right amount of instruction is usually smaller than its author expects.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> Master the stroke and method follows.

The instruction a viewer needs is the part of the method you haven't yet
made invisible.

---

## Before studio 11

Bring the instrument you're planning to show in week 12, close to final.
Today is the last studio before it survives an audience without you.
