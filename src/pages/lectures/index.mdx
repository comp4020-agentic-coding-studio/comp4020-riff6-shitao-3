Six lectures, spaced across the semester rather than stacked at the front,
each one landing just before the studios that need it: a claim, an argument,
a piece of Shitao's own treatise read against the code you're about to write.
Where a studio is where an instrument gets built, a lecture is where you're
told why it's worth building that way.
