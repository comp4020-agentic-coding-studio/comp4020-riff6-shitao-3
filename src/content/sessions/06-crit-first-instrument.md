## Before the studio

Submit First Instrument before the studio starts, per the
[assessment page](/assessments/first-instrument/). Bring it running on a
machine you don't control, if you can borrow one --- your own laptop already
knows how to use your instrument, which is exactly the thing this studio is
checking for.

## In the studio

Full crit format: everyone's instrument gets handed to someone who has never
seen it, with no instructions given out loud. What breaks in that silence is
the actual result of five weeks' work, not the demo you'd give if you were
standing next to it explaining. Marking happens after the studio, against what
was shown here and what's in the repository, not against a later polish pass.

## Afterwards

Whatever the room found in the first thirty seconds of silent handling is the
most useful single fact you'll get about the build all semester. Write it down
before the next studio's constraint replaces it in your head.

## Stretch goal

If you can borrow a phone or tablet before the crit, hand your instrument to
it even if you built for a mouse. It's an early, informal version of the
many-hands test week 7 formalises, and it's a much better place to discover a
touch-only failure than the crit itself.
